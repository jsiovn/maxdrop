# UVW Map Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Evaluate the UVW Map modifier (planar, box, cylindrical with cap, spherical) into the mesh's map channel,
so that walls and floors without stored UVs get the mapping the artist set.

**Architecture:** `maxdrop/convert/uvwmap.py` (no bpy; numpy when called) reads the modifier's old ParamBlock
(slot 1) and Real-World Map Size, evaluates its gizmo (the PRS controller at slot 0) through
`reader/controllers.py`, and writes one UVW per face corner into the channel of a copy of a reader `MeshData`.
`convert/scene.py` applies every UVW Map that renders and sits in the bottom run of the stack that MaxDrop evaluates
(the base mesh, or a shape's Extrude, then UVW Maps): the mesh key gains `(modifier index, context matrix)` per
applied UVW Map, `node.applied` names it. A UVW Map above a modifier that is not evaluated stays listed (an issue);
shrink-wrap, face and XYZ-to-UVW keep the stored UVs (an issue). The oracle (version 5) dumps the evaluated map
channel of every node with a UVW Map: per triangle corner the world position, the face normal and the UVW.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11 syntax, standard library + numpy, unittest; 3ds Max 2020 via 3dsmax-mcp for the probes,
the fixture and the oracle; Blender 4.2 / 4.5 / 5.2 headless for the build.

## What 3ds Max 2020 does (probed on scratch scenes, every formula within 6e-6 of Max's map vertices)

- ClassID `(0xF72B1, 0)`, superclass `0x810`. References `[PRS gizmo (0x2005), old ParamBlock (8)]`. Block index:
  0 map type (0 planar, 1 cylindrical, 2 spherical, 3 shrink-wrap, 4 box, 5 face, 6 XYZ to UVW), 1-3 U/V/W tile,
  4-6 U/V/W flip, 7 cap, 8 channel kind (0 map channel, 1 vertex color), 9 length, 10 width, 11 height, 12
  alignment axis (0 X, 1 Y, 2 Z), 13 map channel, 14 and 15 unknown (0).
- Mapping space: `q = p * context_tm * inverse(gizmo)` (row vectors; the gizmo matrix includes its scale; checked
  with an instanced UVW Map whose two contexts differ). Then a frame (a, b, c) by type and axis:
  - planar, box: axis Z `(-qx, -qy, qz)`, X `(qz, -qy, qx)`, Y `(-qx, qz, qy)`; sizes (width, length, height).
  - cylindrical, spherical: axis Z `(-qx, -qy, qz)`, X `(-qx, -qz, -qy)`, Y `(qz, -qy, qx)`; X = a / (length/2),
    Y = b / (width/2), Z = c / height (cylindrical) or c / (height/2) (spherical).
- Planar: u = a/width + 0.5, v = b/length + 0.5, w = c/height + 0.5; Real-World Map Size drops the 0.5 of u and v
  (only for planar: box, cylindrical and spherical are unchanged).
- Box: the face normal, transformed like the points and divided by the sizes, picks the axis k with the largest
  |component|, s its sign: ±a side u = 0.5 + s·y, v = z + 0.5; ±b side u = 0.5 - s·x, v = z + 0.5; ±c side
  u = 0.5 + s·x, v = y + 0.5; w = the coordinate along k + 0.5 (x, y, z = a/width, b/length, c/height).
- Cylindrical: u = 0.5 + atan2(X, -Y) / 2π, v = c/height + 0.5, w = hypot(X, Y). Cap: a face whose normal (as the
  points, divided by (length/2, width/2, height)) is largest along c: u = 0.5 - Y/2, v = 0.5 + X/2, w = c/height/2.
- Spherical: u as cylindrical, v = 0.5 + asin(Z / r) / π, w = r = |(X, Y, Z)|.
- Then per component: flip -> 1 - value, then times its tile.
- Seam: Max's own vertex sharing puts a whole-number shift on U of some faces (also faces that do not cross the
  seam); MaxDrop shifts a face with an edge across the seam half-plane (X = 0, Y > 0) by +1 on its corners below
  0.5, so no face smears (a first rule, "U spans more than 0.5", was wrong on wide faces). The test compares U modulo
  a whole turn per face for cylindrical and spherical, and leaves out U on a face that goes around the axis.
