# VRay2SidedMtl translucency Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A VRay2SidedMtl whose translucency is not black (or whose back material is on) is drawn as its front
material with a Translucent BSDF mixed in by the translucency weight, and a back material on back faces.

**Architecture:** convert/materials/: `MaterialIR` gets `translucency` (an Input, the weight) and
`translucency_color` (an Input); `("translucency", DATA)` joins `texmaps.SLOT_KINDS`; a new rule
`vray_2sided_mtl` (layered.py) copies the front's IR, sets the weight (the swatch rule on the mean of the
translucency color; with a map, a DATA graph mixing the weight and the map by the multiplier) and appends a
`Layer(mode=BACK)` for a back material that is set, on and not the front. The peel keeps a VRay2SidedMtl without
either (`MaterialConverter._mixes_layers`). build/materials/: `_Tree.fill` mixes a Translucent BSDF (through the
alpha) over its top by the weight (label `Translucency`); `_Tree.layered` mixes a BACK layer by Geometry >
Backfacing.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11, Blender 4.2 / 4.5 / 5.2 shader nodes.

Bead: maxdrop-o53.1.7. Base: `ab6a5c8`.

---

## Decisions (bd notes)

- Missing translucency (param 7) is black: the material stays a peel.
- Leaves the peel when the front is set and not directly a Multi/Sub-Object and (translucency not black, or a
  translucency map set, on, at a multiplier > 0, or a back layer).
- A front that is directly a Multi/Sub-Object: peel kept, a report line. Through a wrapper: first entry, a line.
- The back layer gets the same weight, colored by the front's base color.
- The Translucent goes through the front's alpha (Mix from a Transparent BSDF by the alpha).
- Rider (Double Sided) cut; mult_by_front_diffuse on is a report line.

## Parameters (VRay2SidedMtl block 0, vray_wrappers oracle)

0 frontMtl, 1 backMtl, 3 backMtlOn, 4 texmap_translucency, 5 texmap_translucency_on, 6
texmap_translucency_multiplier (percent), 7 translucency (color), 8 force1SidedSubMtls, 9 mult_by_front_diffuse.
