# Arc: the knot order of a reversed pie Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** give an Arc with both pie and reverse on the knot order 3ds Max 2020 gives it, and prove it, plain and
under an Extrude (side U origin, cap winding), with a self-authored fixture (bead maxdrop-em3.58).

**Architecture:** `maxdrop/reader/shapes.py` `_arc` closes the pie first, then reverses the closed spline keeping
knot 0 first (3ds Max's `Spline3D::Reverse(keepZero)` behaviour); an open arc still reverses whole. A new fixture
`arc_pie_reverse` (reversed pies plain and extruded, a non-reversed extruded pie beside them) is checked by the
existing every-fixture oracle test of `tests/unit/test_shapes.py` (knot by knot) and the eval_mesh test of
`tests/unit/test_extrude.py`; its script also writes `arc_pie_reverse.maps.jsonl` (each extruded node's evaluated
mesh, every corner with its map vertex, object space), which a new test compares corner by corner.

**Tech Stack:** plain Python + numpy (no bpy); MAXScript in 3ds Max 2020 (`3dsmaxbatch.exe` for probes, the dry run
and the oracle; an own interactive `3dsmax.exe -q -silent -U MAXScript <wrapper>` that quits itself for the save);
Blender 4.2 / 4.5 / 5.2 headless for the fixture run.

## Facts (probe 1, 3dsmaxbatch, 2026-11-21; scratch script outside the repo)

Arc radius 10, from 30 to 150, `convertToSplineShape`, knot points (f0 = 30 degrees ... f3 = 150 degrees, C center):

- neither: `f0 f1 f2 f3`, open; f0's in vector and f3's out vector at their knot.
- pie: `f0 f1 f2 f3 C`, closed (as the reader has it).
- reverse: `f3 f2 f1 f0`, open, vectors swapped (as the reader has it).
- **pie and reverse: `f0 C f3 f2 f1`**, closed, every knot's in and out swapped (f0: in = its old out, out = its
  point). The reader gives `f3 f2 f1 f0 C`: the same closed loop the same way round, but starting at f3, not f0.
  That is the closed pie reversed with knot 0 kept first. Probe 2 (pie and reverse, from = to 40, 0 -> 360,
  10 -> 350; and the fixture's 20 -> 130 and 300 -> 60): the same order every time.

## Facts (dry run of the fixture, 3dsmaxbatch, 2026-11-21: corners of the evaluated mesh vs MaxDrop's)

- With `_arc` fixed, FixturePieExt (pie only) and FixtureArcRevExt (reverse only) matched before and after; the
  reversed pies still did not: their outline runs clockwise, and 3ds Max's Extrude turns a clockwise solid (and a
  counter-clockwise hole) by reversing the **whole polyline**: the side U starts at the polyline's last point
  (FixturePieRevExt: U 0 at the arc point just after `from`, `from` itself at 0.9766). MaxDrop kept the first point
  first. A plain clockwise Line (FixtureClockwiseExt: U 0 at its last knot) and a Circle hole (FixtureHoleExt: U 0
  at its last interpolated point) show the same: the rule is general, not the pie's.
- This is a one-rule fix in `convert/extrude.py` (the turn in `extrude()`), outside the bead's Files but needed by
  its Goal (the reversed pie's side U as 3ds Max's); it goes in its own commit. With both fixes every corner of the
  six extruded nodes matches (position, face normal, map vertex, material ID).
