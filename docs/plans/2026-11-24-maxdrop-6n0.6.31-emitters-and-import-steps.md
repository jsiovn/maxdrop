# Emitters and import steps: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** three code-only leftovers of the mesh-light build and the import's steps (bead `maxdrop-6n0.6.31`,
merged from 6n0.6.27, 6n0.6.28 and 6n0.6.1): one extrusion per picked shape, the emitter spread as import steps,
and the pack-cancel check without its race.

**Architecture:**

1. (6n0.6.27) `convert/scene.py`: a `_Extrusions` cache, one per `describe()`, holds per (shape base, Extrude
   index) the reader MeshData or why it is not made, shared by `_extruded` (mesh nodes) and `_emitter_shape`
   (mesh lights that pick a shape under an Extrude). The shape is read through `_shape` (shared `shape_of`, which
   now keeps the refusal text), its shape and Extrude issues are named once, on the node that makes it, and its
   face corners are charged once against `MAX_BUILT_CORNERS`. When the shape's last node is described the
   MeshData is dropped (as now) but the outcome is kept: a light walked after that extrudes the shape again
   without naming or charging anything. A light whose picked shape has no pending node drops it at its own last
   node. Failure lines keep today's texts: a mesh node gets `extrude: ...` once (also when a light met the
   failure first), a light its `light: not built: ...` line on each node.
2. (6n0.6.28) `build/emitters.py`: `spread` becomes a step generator: it links a shown copy of every emitter
   object to measure, yields `(0, n)` (ops shows "measuring the mesh lights of <file>: 0 / n" before the one
   `view_layer.update()` that evaluates the import), then measures each copy in its own step (evaluated mesh
   made and cleared inside the step) and yields `(done, n)`; copies are removed in `finally` (Esc). Nothing to
   measure: no yield at all. `evaluated_area` stays as a one-object wrapper over the same helpers.
   `ops._scene_steps` drives it with a progress text. The update itself is still one uninterruptible call.
3. (6n0.6.1) `tests/blender/run.py` `check_pack_cancel`: the slowed watch holds the unpack (polling the real
   watch every 5 ms, up to 30 s) until `close()` sets the run's stop event, so the real watch raises
   `archive.Cancelled`; it records that it saw the cancel. The check also asserts `ops._busy() == ""` after joining
   the workers.

**Tech Stack:** plain Python (unittest), Blender Python (headless, isolated profile).
