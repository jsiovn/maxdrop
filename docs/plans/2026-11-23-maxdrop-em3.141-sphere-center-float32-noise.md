# Sphere center under a turned gizmo: 3ds Max's float32 noise (decision) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** decide bead maxdrop-em3.141 from the em3.110 probe data: MaxDrop does not emulate 3ds Max 2020's
single-precision point for a spherical UVW Map vertex 1e-7 to 1e-5 gizmo radii off the center under a turned
gizmo, and docs/FORMAT.md says so and why.

**Architecture:** docs only. `maxdrop/convert/uvwmap.py` keeps its float64 point and `CENTER_TOLERANCE = 1e-9`;
the synthetic test `test_the_center_of_a_spherical_gizmo` (tests/unit/test_uvwmap.py) already pins a corner 1e-7
off as the float64 angle. No 3ds Max is started (the bead's notes: closing as not worth matching needs none).

**Tech Stack:** Markdown; plain Python for the unit tests and lint.

## Facts (from docs/plans/2026-11-22-maxdrop-em3.110-uvw-pole-cases.md, Facts 3, and the U / V sizes from the bead's Goal, the em3.110 probes as reported; no new probe)

- 3ds Max takes the center (V 0.5, U the face mean) only where its single-precision point is exactly zero; a
  push of 1e-20 radii (untilted) already gives the angle. MaxDrop's float64 residue of the same vertex is about
  2e-16 radii, so below 1e-9 radii it is the center (FixtureUVWPoleCenter matches).
- Under a turned gizmo, a vertex 1e-7 to 1e-5 radii off gets an angle from 3ds Max's float32 rounding: U off by
  up to about 0.1, V by up to about 0.15 from the float64 point's angle.

## Decision: not matched

- The angle of a point 1e-7 to 1e-5 radii from the center is ill-conditioned: the float32 rounding of a point
  near the gizmo's size (about 6e-8 relative per operation, accumulated through the transform) is not negligible
  against its distance from the center (of its order at 1e-7 radii). 3ds Max's value there comes from its own rounding, not from the scene; the float64
  angle is the one the stored vertex and gizmo describe.
- Matching it would mean a second, single-precision gizmo transform in 3ds Max's operation order (node TM,
  gizmo TM, inverse, the point), which the probes never determined: new probes, and a path that could move the
  pole and center cases uvwmap_pole already pins corner for corner.
- Cost of not matching: a few corners of the fan around such a vertex (U up to ~0.1, V up to ~0.15); the vertex
  must sit within 1e-5 radii of a turned spherical gizmo's center without being on it, which is rare.
