# UVW Map: corners on the axis under a turned gizmo Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** give the corners on (or within about 1e-4 gizmo radii of) the axis of a cylindrical or spherical UVW Map
the U 3ds Max 2020 gives them, also under a turned gizmo, and prove it with a self-authored fixture (bead
maxdrop-em3.57).

**Architecture:** `maxdrop/convert/uvwmap.py` computes a per-kind pole mask in `apply()` and passes it to `_seam`:
cylindrical poles get the fixed U 0.5 (a whole turn up on a face that crosses the seam, as the other corners are),
spherical poles keep the face mean. A new fixture `uvwmap_pole` (Editable Mesh cylinders and spheres, the turn baked
into the mesh and the gizmo, so the axis vertices sit on the gizmo axis with float residues) is checked corner by
corner, poles included, by a new test in `tests/unit/test_uvwmap.py`.

**Tech Stack:** plain Python + numpy (no bpy); MAXScript in 3ds Max 2020 (`3dsmaxbatch.exe` for probes, the dry run
and the oracle; an own interactive `3dsmax.exe -q -silent -U MAXScript <wrapper>` that quits itself for the save);
Blender 4.2 / 4.5 / 5.2 headless for the fixture run.

## Facts (probes 1-5, 3dsmaxbatch, 2026-11-21; scratch scripts outside the repo)

Editable Mesh Cylinder (r 8, h 12, 10 sides, cap centers on the axis) and Sphere (r 8, 10 segments, poles on the
axis); UVW Map length = width = 16, cap off; pole pushed off the axis by a fraction of the gizmo radius:

- **Cylindrical**: an axis corner gets U = 0.5 on every face, the map vertex shared: no face mean (MaxDrop gave the
  face mean, also at exact zero). Exact zero, a turned gizmo (residues 2e-7..7e-7 of 8) and pushes up to 9.5e-5
  radii: 0.5. 1e-4 and above: the angle. Radial: (7e-5, 7e-5) (|.| 9.9e-5) is a pole, (7.5e-5, 7.5e-5) (1.06e-4)
  is not. X scales with length / 2, Y with width / 2; height does not matter.
- On the seam face 3ds Max has (pole 0.5, 0.05, -0.05): MaxDrop's +1 for corners below 0.5 gives (1.05, 0.95) to
  the others, so the pole must go up by one too (1.5) to keep the same image.
- **Spherical**: an axis corner takes the mean U of its face's other corners (as MaxDrop does), also under a turned
  gizmo. Per axis, relative to r = |(X, Y, Z)|: (9.5e-5, 9.5e-5) is a pole, 1.05e-4 along X is not; with height 32
  (Z = 0.5 at the pole) the bound halves (4.5e-5 pole, 5.5e-5 not), with height 160 it is 1e-5.
- The bound is about 1e-4 (bracketed 9.5e-5 / 1.05e-4); exactly at 1e-4 the float32 results disagree with each
  other (cylinder +X not a pole, sphere +X a pole, sphere (-X, -Y) not): MaxDrop uses `< 1e-4` in float64. Real
  residues of a turned gizmo are about 1e-7.
