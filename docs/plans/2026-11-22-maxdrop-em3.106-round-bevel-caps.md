# Round bevel caps Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** an open renderable shape with a round section imports capped at both ends, as 3ds Max renders it
(bead maxdrop-em3.106).

**Architecture:** `build/curves.py` sets `use_fill_caps` whenever the curve has a bevel (round, `bevel_depth > 0`,
or the rectangular section object), not only for the section. `tests/blender/run.py` `check_curve` checks the caps
of every open spline of a round renderable shape against the regular polygon of the bevel ring, and that a closed
spline gets none.

**Tech Stack:** Blender Python (4.2, 4.5, 5.2), the committed fixtures `shapes.max` and `shapes_more.max`.

---

## Measured before the change (Blender 5.2, a scratch probe; no 3ds Max)

Blender's caps of a round bevel are `n - 2` triangles over their own ring of `n = 4 + 2 * bevel_resolution`
vertices (3ds Max's sides), centered on the spline's end point at radius `bevel_depth`: their area is the regular
n-gon, `n / 2 * r^2 * sin(2 pi / n)`.

| Curve | splines | polygons / vertices without caps | with caps | each open end |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FixtureLine (shapes, 8 sides) | closed + open | 240 / 248 | 252 / 264 | 6 triangles, area = 8-gon |
| FixtureCurveStd (12 sides) | closed | 336 / 336 | 336 / 336 (unchanged) | - |
| FixtureCurveMulti (12 sides) | open | 168 / 180 | 188 / 204 | 10 triangles, area = 12-gon |
| FixtureCurveMixed (12 sides) | open | 252 / 264 | 272 / 288 | 10 triangles |
| FixtureCurveIds (12 sides) | 2 open | 336 / 360 | 376 / 408 | 10 triangles |

FixtureLine's delta (12 polygons) is exactly the two caps of its open spline: its closed spline is unchanged.
That 3ds Max caps a round section rests on the probe cited by the bead (maxdrop-em3.48: a Line, round, 4 sides:
8 vertices, 12 triangles); not measured again here (code lane: no 3ds Max).

## Task 1: the cap check (red first)

**Files:** Modify `tests/blender/run.py` (`check_curve`, the header docstring's shapes sentence).

- [ ] In `check_curve`, after the bevel check of a round renderable shape: per spline, faces of the evaluated mesh
  whose every vertex lies within `r * (1 + 1e-6)` of the spline's first and last point (object space). Open spline:
  at each end the summed area equals the n-gon within 1e-6 relative (`n = shape["sides"]`). Closed spline: no such
  face at its first point. Label `"<label> spline <k>: capped at both ends ..."` / `"... closed: no cap"`.
