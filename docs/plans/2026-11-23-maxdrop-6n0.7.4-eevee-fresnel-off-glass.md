# EEVEE gap of Fresnel-off glass Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** say why Fresnel-off glass (maxdrop-6n0.7.2's mirror over the refraction twin) is darker in EEVEE than in
Cycles, measured, in `docs/SUPPORT.md` and the `build/materials.py` docstring; the drawing stays.

**Architecture:** a first measurement (hand-wired nodes, Blender 5.2, the rotated white cube, white world) shows
the loss is not total internal reflection: a lone Refraction BSDF keeps 0.994 in EEVEE, a lone white metal 0.999,
but Mix(w)[Refraction, metal] gives w + (1 - w)^2 (w 0.2: 0.835, w 0.5: 0.748), and a Refraction BSDF of color 0.8
gives 0.636 = 0.8^2: EEVEE applies a refraction closure's weight times color twice through a solid (entry and
exit), the mix factor included, and has no internal bounce to return the share the mirror reflects back inside.
Cycles' internal bounces give w + (1 - w) T. A tree that lifts EEVEE (a refraction weight or color above the mix's)
lifts Cycles by the same amount, since both read the same sockets; the one drawing that would keep Cycles is a
second Material Output with target EEVEE, which duplicates the stack above every such BSDF (layers too) and every
"the output node" lookup: out of scope for a P4 bead. So: documented, not redrawn.

**Tech Stack:** Blender 4.2 / 4.5 / 5.2 headless (Cycles, EEVEE), plain Python unittest.
