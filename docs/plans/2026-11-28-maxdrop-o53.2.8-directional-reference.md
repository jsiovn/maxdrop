# Directional reference: a VRayLight's directional against an area light's spread Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [x]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** a measured rule in docs/FORMAT.md "VRayLight" that maxdrop-o53.3.6 needs: a VRayLight's directional
(parameter 36) as Blender's area-light spread plus a power correction, with the V-Ray and the Cycles tables, and
what EEVEE does with spread on 4.2, 4.5 and 5.2.

**Architecture:** 3dsmaxbatch renders the calibration floor ("VRayLight", Calibration) under a VRayLight plane
and a disc 1 m above it at several directional values and saves the directional-0 scenes; headless Blender imports
those scenes with MaxDrop (the same light, Light Strength 1), puts a Diffuse BSDF 1.0 on the floor and renders the
area light at a sweep of spreads in Cycles (5.2) and at two spreads in EEVEE (4.2, 4.5, 5.2). A scratch fit
script reads both result files and fits spread(directional) and the correction. Scratch files stay in
a scratch folder of the bead's own (outside the repo). No `maxdrop/` code; nothing committed but this plan and
docs/FORMAT.md.

**Tech Stack:** MAXScript (3ds Max 2020, V-Ray 5.2, `3dsmaxbatch.exe`), Blender 4.2.23 / 4.5.16 / 5.2 headless
with an isolated profile, plain Python.

Bead: maxdrop-o53.2.8 · Base: 233ad14

## Task 1: V-Ray probe

**Files:** `<scratch>\probe_vray.ms`, `result_vray.txt`, `plane_d0.max`, `disc_d0.max`

- [x] Scene (millimeters): the calibration floor (white VRayMtl 20 m square, no reflection), V-Ray linear color
  mapping, gamma 1, clamp off, no GI, no default lights, 3ds Max gamma off. The light 1 m above the floor, node
  rotation identity (shines down), invisible, units W, multiplier 10, subdivs raised for noise: a plane of size0 =
  size1 = 100 mm (200 × 200) and a disc of size0 100 mm (one at a time).
- [x] Camera: a Freecamera of identity rotation 0.5 m above the floor, FOV 2 · atan(2.2) = 131.098° (± 1.1 m
  seen), a 440 × 440 float bitmap (5 mm per pixel); a self-lit marker at +X 1000 mm checks the mapping.
- [x] Per render: the mean of the 8 × 8 pixels centered on floor points 0, 270 (15.1°), 575 (29.9°) and 1000 mm
  (45°) from the center along +X, −X, +Y and −Y (four samples per angle: the noise); a wide frame (camera 2 m up,
  ± 9 m seen, 100 mm per pixel) whose pixel sum is the flux on the floor (a Lambertian emitter puts 98.8 % of it
  inside 9 m).
- [x] Filter off, bucket sampler (fine subdivs 32, threshold 0.002), local subdivs on, light subdivs 64.
- [x] Directional 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9 for both shapes in W; the plane in default units at 0, 0.5, 0.9 (does
  the factor depend on the units family); save the directional-0 W scene of each shape.
