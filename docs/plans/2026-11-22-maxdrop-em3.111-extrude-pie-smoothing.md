## The probe (done, 2026-11-22)

Scratch MAXScript under `3dsmaxbatch`: closed Lines (a 20 x 20 square with a fifth knot A on its bottom edge, at
(10, 0) straight or (10, -4) kinked) and two open ones, each under Extrude (1 segment, smooth on), the hard edges of
the evaluated mesh counted as the oracle counts them and placed by position.

| knot A | breaks |
| --- | --- |
| corner (kinked, straight, a 2.3 degree kink) | yes |
| smooth (kinked by line segments, straight, curved) | no |
| Bezier corner (collinear or not) | yes |
| Bezier, both vectors non-zero (also where line segments kink the spline) | no |
| Bezier, in or out vector equal to the point (kinked or straight, curve or line segments, open spline too) | yes |
| Bezier, in vector 1e-3 .. 1e-6 from the point at x = 10, 1e-9 from it at the origin | no |
| Bezier, in vector 1e-7 from the point at x = 10 (the same float32) | yes |

Rule: a corner or Bezier-corner knot, or a Bezier knot whose in or out vector is exactly its point (float32
equality, no tolerance). 3ds Max re-aligns a Bezier knot's handles (`setInVec` / `setOutVec` made non-collinear
come back collinear), so a non-collinear Bezier knot cannot be made in 3ds Max. The pie's from (in = point), center
(both) and to (out = point) are such knots.

Side finding (not this bead): a curve segment whose in vector lies 1e-3 or 1e-4 off the chord is straight for 3ds
Max's Optimize (no steps), curved for `_straight` (6 steps): maxdrop-em3.142 under maxdrop-em3.
