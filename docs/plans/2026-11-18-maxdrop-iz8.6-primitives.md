# Primitives by Parameter Id Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build Box, Plane, Pyramid, Sphere, GeoSphere, Cylinder, Cone, Tube and Torus from their parameter blocks as
the meshes 3ds Max makes, instead of the placeholders M1 left (Teapot stays a placeholder).

**Architecture:** `maxdrop/convert/primitives.py` (no bpy; numpy for the arrays) reads the primitive's ParamBlock2
(reference slot 0) by parameter id and its Real-World Map Size flag, and builds a `reader.geometry.MeshData` in object
space and file units: the TriMesh 3ds Max builds (`o.baseObject.mesh`), its triangles merged into the polygons 3ds
Max shows (two triangles across a hidden edge are a quad, cap fans stay triangles around their center vertex, so the
vertex and triangle counts are 3ds Max's), smoothing groups, zero-based material IDs and, with Generate Mapping Coords,
map channel 1. `convert/scene.py` asks it for every base object that is not an Editable Mesh or Poly; the MeshData then
goes the way of any mesh (offset, UVW Map, converted modifiers, instancing). Refused options (Slice, a hemisphere)
keep the placeholder with an issue.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11 syntax, standard library + numpy, unittest; 3ds Max 2020 through 3dsmax-mcp for the
probes, the fixture and the oracle; Blender 4.2 / 4.5 / 5.2 headless.

---

## What 3ds Max 2020 stores and builds (probed: about 80 primitives, compared vertex for vertex)

- ClassIds (classA, classB), superclass 0x10: Box (0x10, 0), Sphere (0x11, 0), Cylinder (0x12, 0), Torus (0x20, 0),
  Cone (0xA86C23DD, 0), Tube (0x7B21, 0), GeoSphere (0, 0x7F9E), Plane (0x081F1DFC, 0x77566F65), Pyramid
  (0x76BF318A, 0x4BF37B10).
- One ParamBlock2 in reference slot 0. Ids: Box 0 length, 1 width, 2 height, 3 width segs, 4 length segs, 5 height
  segs, 6 mapping; Plane 0 length, 1 width, 2 width segs, 3 length segs, 4 density, 5 render scale, 6 mapping;
  Pyramid 0 width, 1 depth, 2 height, 3-5 segs, 6 mapping; Sphere 0 radius, 1 segs, 2 smooth, 3 hemisphere, 4
  squash, 5 base to pivot, 6 mapping, 7 slice, 8-9 slice angles; GeoSphere 0 radius, 1 segs, 2 base type, 3
  hemisphere, 4 smooth, 5 base to pivot, 6 mapping; Cylinder 0 radius, 1 height, 2 height segs, 3 cap segs, 4
  sides, 5 smooth, 6 slice, 7-8 angles, 9 mapping; Cone 0-1 radii, 2 height, 3 height segs, 4 cap segs, 5 sides, 6
  smooth, 7 slice, 8-9, 10 mapping; Tube 0-1 radii, 2 height, 3 sides, 4 cap segs, 5 height segs, 6 smooth, 7
  slice, 8-9, 10 mapping; Torus 0-1 radii, 2 rotation, 3 twist (radians), 4 segs, 5 sides, 6 smooth (0 none, 1
  alternating by side, 2 all, 3 alternating by segment), 7 slice, 8-9, 10 mapping.
- Real-World Map Size: `0x2150{0x0100 u32 count, 0x0110{0x0120 class, 0x0130 u32 flag}}` in the object's chunk (no
  0x2150 with mapping off).
- The per-class topology, material IDs, smoothing groups and UV rules are in the module's docstrings and
  docs/FORMAT.md ("Primitives"). Facts worth keeping: a Box's role (ID and UV direction) moves to the opposite layer
  under negative sizes, its faces point in when width and length differ in sign; a cylinder's U starts at 0.75 on
  +X and its seam shifts a face down a whole turn when it would reach 1, its caps take the side's U (degenerate)
  unless real-world (flat over the rim's extent); cap rings take the other cap's material ID; GeoSphere subdivides
  each base triangle in rows of equal angles (slerp), its U per face from atan2 with the corners past 3/4 brought
  round when a face spans more than a half turn; a point on the -X meridian takes U 0 or 1 by float rounding in 3ds
  Max itself (not reproduced).

## Outcome

- About 90 probe primitives and the 26-node fixture equal 3ds Max 2020's TriMesh polygon for polygon (positions,
  winding, IDs, groups, UVs), but for a GeoSphere point on the -X meridian whose U 3ds Max's own rounding decides.

  is 2^21 face corners (1.5 to 4 s and 450 MB at the cap, measured); the GeoSphere caches its lattice; the fixture
  gained real-world nodes for every class, a Pyramid and a Tube of negative height (which found the Tube's rule:
  the radii trade roles, no flip) and a Sphere of odd segments with Base To Pivot; the refusal path through
  convert.scene and a hostile-size sweep are tested.
