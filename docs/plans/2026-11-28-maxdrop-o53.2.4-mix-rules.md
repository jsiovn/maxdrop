# The mix rules of VRayBlendMtl, VRay2SidedMtl and Blend, measured in V-Ray 5.2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [x]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** formulas in docs/FORMAT.md "Materials and maps" for the weight of a VRayBlendMtl coat (grey, colored, a
blend map with its multiplier, additive), the front and back light a VRay2SidedMtl translucency shows, and a Blend's
mix amount, mask and Use Curve, each measured in V-Ray 5.2 with the scene's gamma off and on.

**Architecture:** 3dsmaxbatch probes (in a probe folder of this bead's own, outside the repo) render a 20 m plane
carrying the material under test, lit by standard Omnis without decay 1 m in front of and / or behind it, through a
camera at 0.9 m looking down: V-Ray linear color mapping, gamma 1, clamp off, no GI, no default lights, into an HDR
bitmap read with `getPixels ... linear:true` (mean of the 8 x 8 center pixels). Every run renders control rows in
its gamma mode: a VRayLightMtl white (1.0) and grey 128 (0.502), a white VRayMtl (E, the lit reference). Shares are
ratios to E. A scratch Python script recomputes every row from the formulas (Verify: within 2 %). No `maxdrop/` code.

**Tech Stack:** MAXScript (3ds Max 2020, V-Ray 5.2, `3dsmaxbatch.exe`), plain Python.
