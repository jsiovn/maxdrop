# Extrude: no cap on a self-crossing outline Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A closed spline whose own edges cross, touch or run over each other gets no cap under an Extrude, morph or
grid, as in 3ds Max 2020; it is no hole or container for the other splines and keeps its own direction (bead
maxdrop-em3.140).

**Architecture:** `convert/extrude.py` tests each closed polyline for two non-adjacent edges that meet (an exact 2D
segment test on XY, a sweep over the edges' x ranges in numpy, under a budget), and marks a polyline that lost a
zero-length segment between straight edges (`Polyline.doubled`). Those polylines leave the closed set before
nesting: no cap, not turned, not counted for the grid. One counted report line per mesh.

**Tech Stack:** Python + numpy (convert/, no bpy), MAXScript in 3ds Max 2020 (`3dsmaxbatch`) for the probes.

Bead: maxdrop-em3.140

---

## Facts (3ds Max 2020, `3dsmaxbatch`, `snapshotAsMesh`, 5 probe runs, outside the repo)

Amount 2, 1 segment, both caps; counts are vertices / faces (triangles).

- **Crossing, touching, overlapping**: no cap, morph and grid alike, only the sides (Bowtie 8 / 8, Pentagram 10 / 10,
  the slit 14 / 14, an edge run back over the one before 14 / 14, a vertex exactly on another vertex 16 / 16 or on
  another edge 10 / 10, a Bezier rectangle whose third knot moved without its vectors 32 / 32).
- **Exact, on XY**: a vertex 1e-6 above the edge at x 10 is capped (10 / 16), 1e-6 across it is not; 1e-3 above at a
  scale of 20000 capped. Crossings in XY at other heights (z 0 and 5) drop the cap too.
- **Adjacent edges never count**: a closed outline of three points on a line (all edges adjacent) is capped (6 / 8).
  Straight runs of points along a side are capped (Collinear 10 / 16).
- **Per spline**: the other splines of the shape keep their caps (a bowtie beside a square 16 / 20); a crossing spline
  is no hole (a bowtie in a 40 square: the square capped whole, 2 triangles a cap; grid 898 = 16 + 2 x 441) and no
  container (a square in a bowtie's lobe is a solid, area 50). Crossings between two splines change nothing
  (TwoCrossing 16 / 24, grid 1780); an open spline that crosses itself or a closed one changes nothing.
- **Direction**: a crossing spline is not turned: its sides run from its first knot in its own direction (SlitCW and
  BowtieCW: every side corner, position and UV, as the spline runs), like an open spline.
- **Zero-length segments** (two knots on one spot one after the other): 3ds Max keeps both points (DupCorner 10 / 10
  where MaxDrop merges them: 8). Between straight edges the cap goes, at any coordinates (DupCorner, DupMid, the last
  knot on the first, a triangle with a doubled corner, a rounded Rectangle at steps 0). Between interpolated curves
  (a rounded Rectangle with its corner radius half a side, steps 6) 3ds Max's own rounding decides: 10 x 20 r 5 and
  square 14.6 and 26 capped, 7.3 x 19.1, 3 x 4, 100 x 250, square 10, 40 not; the 10 x 20 pill at steps 1 to 7 and 12
  capped, 10 not. MaxDrop caps those (merged points, no straight edges around them).
- **Optimize and a zero chord**: a curve segment from a knot to another on the same spot gets no steps under Optimize
  (the 10 x 20 r 5 pill: 32 points, MaxDrop 44), its steps without Optimize (56).
