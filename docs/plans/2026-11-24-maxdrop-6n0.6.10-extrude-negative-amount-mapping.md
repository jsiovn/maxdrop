# Extrude: a negative amount's V and lone caps (maxdrop-6n0.6.10) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** An Extrude with a negative amount maps its sides and places a lone cap as 3ds Max 2020 does: V runs from
the far level, and Cap Start is the cap at the far level.

**Architecture:** `convert/extrude.py` `_sides` gives each level's V as (segments - level) / segments on a negative
amount (level 0 at the shape), times the signed amount with Real-World Map Size. `extrude()` puts Cap Start at the
last level and Cap End at level 0 when the amount is negative; each cap faces out (+Z at the top level, -Z at the
bottom), which replaces the "lone cap faces as upwards, into the solid" rule.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11 (no bpy), numpy, unittest.

---

## Probe (3ds Max 2020, 3dsmaxbatch, 2026-11-24; scratch folder outside the repo)

A 20 x 10 rectangle (line corners) unless named, morph caps, Generate Mapping Coordinates and Material IDs on;
every 3ds Max triangle's corners (object-space position and UVW, 4 decimals) looked up in MaxDrop's faces.

- Sides, negative amount: V 0 at the far level (z = amount), 1 at the shape, (segments - level) / segments
  between (amount -5, segments 1, 2, 3; amount -0.75, segments 4); U unchanged. Real-World Map Size: that V times
  the signed amount: -5 at the shape, 0 at the far level (-5 / 3: -5, -3.3333, -1.6667, 0). Positive: V = level /
  segments, times the amount (unchanged; PosS1, PosS3RW match today). The same on an open spline, a clockwise
  outline (turned), a hole, a Circle, a shape at z 3 (V by level, not by z), both caps off.
- Caps, negative amount: Cap Start lies at the far level, faces -Z, ID 2; Cap End at the shape, faces +Z, ID 1,
  alone or together (rectangle, hole, clockwise, z 3; grid caps too: the grid points at z 0 + the level's rise,
  Cap Start's at z = amount). MaxDrop today puts a lone Cap Start at the shape facing -Z (into the solid) and a
  lone Cap End at the far level facing +Z: the face direction and ID were right (em3's probe saw only those), the
  level was not. The caps' U / V: the shape's XY box, as today (the both-caps cases already match).
- Positive lone caps: unchanged (MPosStartRW matches).
