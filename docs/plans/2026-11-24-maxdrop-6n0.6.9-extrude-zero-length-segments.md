# Extrude: zero-length segments (maxdrop-6n0.6.9) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Settle the bead's three questions about a spline's zero-length segment under an Extrude: keep merging its
two points (decision written in docs/FORMAT.md), leave the pill's cap as it is (the 3ds Max probe found no rule
MaxDrop can compute), and fix the one defect the probe found: a doubled knot between straight segments without
Optimize is capped by MaxDrop, not by 3ds Max.

**Architecture:** `convert/extrude.py` `_polyline` marks a closed spline `doubled` when a coincident run of points
sits between two straight edges. Today "straight" is read as "no steps follow the point" (`bare`), which only holds
under Optimize; without it a straight segment keeps its steps and the flag is missed. The flag becomes the segment's
own straightness (`flat`: a line segment, or a curve `_straight` calls straight), judged once per run of coincident
points (the edge into the run and the edge out of it, wrapping round a closed spline), and a zero-length straight
segment's steps are its point exactly (no float jitter that defeats the exact merge).

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11 (no bpy), numpy, unittest.

## Probe (the bead's Scope bullet 3), 3ds Max 2020 3dsmaxbatch, 2026-11-24

Scratch folder outside the repo. Results:

- Pill knots (Pill_10x20, 7.3x19.1, 3x4, Full_7.3, Full_5, steps 7 and 10): bit-identical to `shapes._rectangle`'s
  (0 ulp); the two knots on each doubled spot bit-identical in 3ds Max, and its two mesh vertices there too, in
  capped and uncapped pills alike: the cap is not decided by the knots.
- Interpolated step points: MaxDrop's (float64, then float32) differ from 3ds Max's by 1 to 4 ulps (3ds Max's are
  not even mirror-symmetric). Whatever test 3ds Max runs at the doubled spot, its outcome hangs on those bits:
  no rule MaxDrop can compute. Caps stay as they are; docs/FORMAT.md says so.
- Doubled knot without Optimize (steps 6): DupCorner 70 / 70, DupMid 84 / 84, CloseDup (last knot on the first)
  70 / 70: every point kept, no cap. Steps 0: 10 / 10, 12 / 12, 10 / 10. MaxDrop before this bead: DupCorner and
  DupMid uncapped only by float jitter of the zero-length segment's steps, CloseDup **capped** (56 / 108).

## Revision after review (2026-11-24)

The review asked for a probe of the cases the per-run rule changed under Optimize. Second probe (steps 6): a line
into a doubled spot and a curve out, two or three knots: no cap; a curve in and a line out: no cap; curves on both
sides, two or three knots: capped; three line knots: no cap; curves 1 % off their chord around a doubled line knot:
no cap with and without Optimize. The rule is "a straight edge into **or** out of the run", not "and";
`test_a_straight_edge_on_either_side_of_a_doubled_knot` holds those cases, FORMAT.md the rows.
