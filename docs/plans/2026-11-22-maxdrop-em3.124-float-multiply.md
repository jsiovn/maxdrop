# Multiply of two maps in a float graph: the product of the means, as V-Ray Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** decide by a V-Ray 5.2 render whether a Multiply of two colored maps in a float graph (a mask, a data slot)
is the mean of the product or the product of the means, and make MaxDrop's drawing and its docstring say what V-Ray
does.

**Architecture:** `MaterialBuilder.multiply()` (build/materials.py) draws a Multiply in FLOAT as a Math MULTIPLY of
the two sides, each a float graph and so already through `graph()`'s mean (DOT_PRODUCT by 1/3): mean(a) x mean(b).
The measurement below shows V-Ray does the same, so the code stays; the docstring's "Data maps" paragraph states the
measured rule, and a builder check pins the drawing so a later "fix" towards mean(a x b) fails.

**Tech Stack:** 3ds Max 2020 + V-Ray 5.2 (`3dsmaxbatch`, scratch MAXScript), Blender 4.2.23 / 4.5.16 / 5.2 headless,
plain Python `unittest`.

---

## Measurement (outside the repository, a probe folder of the bead's own)

`probe.ms` (3dsmaxbatch): two stripe images, 0 / 255 per channel (so the gamma plays no part), eight stripes each:
A = R R Y W Y R W M, B = G R C R M C W B. Rows: a VRayLightMtl black -> white Mix masked by an RGB Multiply (A, B);
a VRayMtl whose reflection glossiness map is that RGB Multiply (VFB channel VRayMtlReflectGlossiness through
`vfbControl #setchannel` / `#saveimage`); a VRayLightMtl Mix masked by a CoronaMix Multiply (A, B), which V-Ray
evaluates too. `blender_render.py` imports the saved scene through MaxDrop (this checkout) and draws what feeds each
mask factor and Roughness (1 - x for the glossiness row) as an emission; EEVEE and Cycles, median per stripe.

| Stripe (A x B) | R x G | R x R | Y x C | W x R | Y x M | R x C | W x W | M x B |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mean(A x B) | 0 | 0.333 | 0.333 | 0.333 | 0.333 | 0 | 1 | 0.333 |
| mean(A) x mean(B) | 0.111 | 0.111 | 0.444 | 0.333 | 0.444 | 0.222 | 1 | 0.222 |
| V-Ray: RGB Multiply mask, glossiness; CoronaMix Multiply mask | 0.111 | 0.111 | 0.444 | 0.333 | 0.444 | 0.222 | 1 | 0.222 |
| MaxDrop (4.2.23, 4.5.16, 5.2, EEVEE and Cycles, all three rows) | 0.111 | 0.111 | 0.444 | 0.333 | 0.444 | 0.222 | 1 | 0.222 |

V-Ray takes the product of the means (3ds Max's mono value of each side, multiplied); MaxDrop already draws that,
within 0.002 on every row, version and engine. The bead's other branch (the product in color, then one mean) would
be off by up to 0.22: no code change.
