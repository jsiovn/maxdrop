# maxdrop-o53.1.10: two-sided in both engines

Bead: maxdrop-o53.1.10. Base: `29c2e812e4aaa220c4285bdd1ea189e4346236a0`.

**Goal:** the Translucent mix of maxdrop-o53.1.7 shows light from behind in Cycles and in EEVEE on Blender 4.2, 4.5
and 5.2, or the docs say where it does not.

## What the probe found (scratch script, headless, before any code change)

Setup: a 4 x 4 m plane (front +Z), a 2 m area light of 100 W 1 m behind it shining at the back, an orthographic camera
(1 m) 2 m in front, black world, 8 x 8 px, Standard view, Cycles 256 samples (CPU, no denoise), EEVEE 32 samples; the
builder's material, base color 0.8, translucency color 0.8, translucency 0.5 against 0. Mean red:

| Version | Cycles | EEVEE as built at base | EEVEE, Thickness 0 | EEVEE, SLAB, Thickness 0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4.2.23 | 1.6435 | 4.5939 | 1.6287 | 1.6287 |
| 4.5.16 | 1.6435 | 4.6515 | 1.6479 | 1.6479 |
| 5.2.0 | 1.6435 | 0.1213 | 1.6486 | 1.6486 |

translucency 0: 0.0000 everywhere. Thickness mode (SPHERE / SLAB) changes nothing once Thickness is 0: the setting is
a Value of 0 linked into the Material Output's Thickness (all three versions have the socket). At alpha 0.5 (the
builder sets BLENDED): Cycles 0.8222, EEVEE BLENDED 0.05 to 0.20 without, 0.0000 with Thickness 0; DITHERED with
Thickness 0: 0.788 / 0.794 / 0.794. So a translucent material also renders DITHERED.
