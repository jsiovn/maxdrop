# CoronaVolumeMtl and Corona absorption as a Blender volume Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** CoronaVolumeMtl, CoronaPhysicalMtl's volumetric absorption / scattering and CoronaLegacyMtl's absorption /
scattering import as a Blender volume (Volume Absorption + Volume Scatter + Emission on the Material Output's
Volume socket) by one medium rule measured on Corona 12 renders, in place of the grey material and the named issues.

**Architecture:** `maxdrop/convert/materials.py` gets a bpy-free `Medium` (per-channel coefficients per meter) on
`MaterialIR.medium`, made by one function `corona_medium` that the three rules call; CoronaVolumeMtl gets its own
rule (alpha 0: no surface) and leaves CORONA_GREY. `maxdrop/build/materials.py` draws a medium as nodes on the
output's Volume socket (`_Tree.volume`, new).

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.11 (bpy-free convert), Blender 4.2 / 4.5 / 5.2 shader nodes, 3ds Max 2020 + Corona 12
(3dsmaxbatch probes, local, outside the repo).

## Measurements (done before the plan; Corona 12, 3dsmaxbatch, tone mapping off, gamma off, linear readout)

Probe folder: local, outside the repo (bead notes). Slabs (boxes 30 x 30 x h world units) over a CoronaLightMtl plane
of intensity 1 in a black environment, an orthographic camera looking down through them; and in a white furnace
(white environment, no emitter).

- Absorption: transmission = color^(h / distance), exact for grey at every thickness (0.50196 at h = 1 ... 40 of
  distance 10: 0.93339, 0.84172, 0.70849, 0.59634, 0.50195, 0.35563, 0.25196, 0.12647, 0.06348; distance 20 at h 20
  and 40 the same as distance 10 at 10 and 20; stepSize 0.1 changes nothing). A tinted color is exact at h =
  distance; at other thicknesses the channels couple a little (204/128/51 at h 5: 0.899 / 0.714 / 0.455 for
  0.894 / 0.709 / 0.447; at h 20: 0.614 / 0.238 / 0.030 for 0.640 / 0.252 / 0.040). CoronaVolumeMtl,
  CoronaPhysicalMtl (refraction 1, IOR 1) and CoronaLegacyMtl (refraction 1, IOR 1) give the same numbers.
- Scattering: the scattering color is the albedo of the extinction the absorption color and distance make
  (absorption white or distance 0: nothing scatters, the slab is invisible; absorption 128 grey with scattering
  white: furnace 0.997, nothing absorbed). sigma_t = -ln(absorption color) / distance per channel, sigma_s =
  scattering color x sigma_t, sigma_a = (1 - scattering color) x sigma_t. Directionality is a Henyey-Greenstein g.
- Emission: emission color / emission distance per unit length, absorbed on the way out as any light (color
  255/128/64, distance 10: h 5 0.5 / 0.251 / 0.125, h 10 1.0 / 0.502 / 0.251; with absorption 128 grey distance 10:
  0.7226 / 0.3627 / 0.1814 = e / sigma (1 - e^(-sigma h))). Distance 0: none.
- Surface: CoronaVolumeMtl's box has none (no reflection, no refraction: 1.0 over the emitter and in the furnace).
- CoronaLegacyMtl mediaMode (427) 1, the default, is this medium; 0 renders the slab black (not converted, named).
- Single bounce (CoronaVolumeMtl 209, CoronaPhysicalMtl 283, CoronaLegacyMtl 315): 0.598 for 0.674 (no Blender
  equivalent, named).

Cycles (Blender 5.2) of the same slabs with Volume Scatter (color sigma_s / D, density D = max sigma_s), Volume
Absorption (color 1 - sigma_a / D, density D = max sigma_a) and an Emission (color, strength 1 / distance) on the
Volume socket: absorption exact at the grey and at h = distance; scattering 0.681 / 0.566 (Corona 0.683 or 0.674 /
0.570), furnace 0.992 / 0.677 (0.997 / 0.6775), g 0.6 / -0.6: 0.868 / 0.603 (0.863 / 0.6075), furnace g 0.6 0.692
(0.691); emission exact. An unlinked Surface with nothing on the Volume socket renders black in Cycles: a volume-only
material keeps a surface that lets everything through (Principled Alpha 0).
