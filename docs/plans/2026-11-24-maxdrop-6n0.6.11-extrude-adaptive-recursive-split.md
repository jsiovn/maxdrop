# Extrude Adaptive recursive split Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** under Adaptive, `convert/extrude.py` gives a curved segment the points 3ds Max 2020 gives it: a recursive
split at the middle, each piece under the straight test, down to pieces 1/2048 of the segment (maxdrop-6n0.6.11).

**Architecture:** `_adaptive_steps` (uniform depth up to 5, a midpoint test) is replaced by `_adaptive`: the
parameters t (dyadic, sorted) where 3ds Max puts the points, with the points. A piece [a, b] is split at its middle when its points at
a + 0.4 (b - a) and a + 0.6 (b - a) turn the path B(a) -> S -> B(b) by more than 2 degrees (`_bent`, `_straight`'s test on
the piece), and only while it is wider than 2^-11 (`ADAPTIVE_DEPTH` 11). `_polyline` places the points at those t,
not at uniform steps. `_estimate` cannot bound Adaptive by 2047 points a segment without refusing ordinary shapes
of a few hundred knots, so Adaptive is counted while it is made: `polylines(shape, most)` raises ExtrudeError once
the points pass `most` (`extrude()` passes MAX_VERTICES // levels); `_estimate` counts an adaptive segment as 1.

**Tech Stack:** plain Python (`tests/unit`), 3ds Max 2020 `3dsmaxbatch` for the probe (scratch, outside the repo),
Blender 4.2 / 4.5 / 5.2 headless for the smoke tests and `tests/blender/run.py`.

## The probe (done, 2026-11-24)

Scratch under `3dsmaxbatch` (outside the repo, the 6n0_6_11 folder of the local probe scratch): open SplineShapes of
two Bezier-corner knots, steps 6, optimize, adaptive, under an Extrude (1 segment, no caps); every vertex at z 0
printed, so the points' parameters (not only their count) are read back: the split tree is rebuilt from the points
in order (a piece is split exactly when its middle is a point).

- 16 segments of em3.142's table (collinear, off the chord, S, loop, xloop, cusp, hook, zz, big, tight, knee): every
  point sits at a dyadic t, and each tree is the recursive split with the 0.4 / 0.6 two-degree test per piece
  (evaluated on the original curve) with no mismatch in 14 of them. zz: 4 pieces of 255 nodes disagree, each within
  0.1 degree of the 2-degree line (129 points vs 131 in double); tight (a cusp at t 0.5): pieces of 10^-5 chords
  next to the cusp split at 0.5 to 1.9 degrees (73 vs 63 in double).
- What stops the split: a cusp at t 1/3 (never dyadic, the piece holding it always turns 180 degrees): the piece of
  width 1/2048 holding it is kept (cusp13: 49 steps; at 1000x and 0.01x scale 50 and 50). No piece is ever narrower
  than 2^-11: the depth limit is 11.
- The rest is float32: the same curves far from the origin get more points, 3ds Max computing in float32 (h1, s1,
  loop, knee, s2 at +1000: 9, 19, 15, 61, 36 steps; at +10000: 10, 81, 15, 393, 245; cusp13 at +10000: 680; tight at
  +1000: 350; zz at x100: 131). No float32 formula tried reproduced 3ds Max's own point values (best 554 of 673),
  so MaxDrop computes in double and matches 3ds Max on curves near their origin away from cusps.
