# A grey reflection on refracting glass Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** a reflection weight w between 0 and 1 (or a map) on fully or partly refracting glass reflects w x F and
refracts the rest, as V-Ray splits it, instead of the full Fresnel reflection a Principled BSDF keeps whatever its
Specular IOR Level.

**Architecture:** em3.119's Mix Shader (`Reflection weight`, factor w) stays; on a refracting Principled (Transmission
Weight linked or above 0) its first shader, the part without reflection, becomes a Refraction BSDF (label
`Refraction without reflection`: Color the square root of the Base Color, Roughness, IOR and Normal the BSDF's; a
thin wall: IOR 1 and the Base Color as it is), mixed by the Transmission Weight under em3.119's Principled twin (now
also Transmission Weight 0) when the transmission is not a constant of at least 1. The factor becomes max(w, F >
0.9999) for a solid (Fresnel node at the BSDF's IOR and normal): Cycles' Refraction BSDF is black where light is
totally reflected inside the glass. A Principled with an alpha below 1 or linked, an emission, a coat or a sheen keeps
em3.119's twin (the Refraction BSDF has none of them). Fresnel off on full glass keeps the lone BSDF (unchanged; debt).

**Tech Stack:** Blender 4.2 / 4.5 / 5.2 headless (Cycles, EEVEE), plain Python unittest.

## Probes (scratch, this session; one sphere radius 1, IOR 1.6, smooth, uniform white world, orthographic camera)

Mean per band of the sphere radius (0-0.2, 0.4-0.6, 0.8-0.88, 0.9-0.95, 0.8-0.95), Cycles 5.2 (4.2 the same):

| Case | 0-0.2 | 0.8-0.88 | 0.9-0.95 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Principled glass, base black (reflection F only) | 0.0533 | 0.0945 | 0.1663 |
| E opaque, base black, level 0.5 | 0.0533 | 0.0945 | 0.1663 |
| F Mix 0.502 [Refraction black, A] | 0.0267 | 0.0475 | 0.0835 (= 0.502 x A) |
| B Principled glass, base 0.25 | 0.2899 | 0.3210 | 0.3747 |
| C Refraction color 0.25 / D color 0.5 | 0.0625 / 0.2500 | flat | flat |

EEVEE (5.2, 4.2): A = E, F = 0.502 x A, C 0.0625, D 0.25: both engines tint twice through a solid (once per
interface), the Principled by sqrt(base) per interface, so the twin's color is sqrt(Base Color). Thin wall (5.2 only):
Cycles tints each wall by the base color (Refraction IOR 1 color c: 0.0625 for c 0.25), EEVEE a closed thin-walled
object once: the twin follows Cycles; a clear glass (white) is the same in both. A Principled thin wall at IOR 1 is no
twin (dark at grazing in both engines). Rough (0.3): the Refraction BSDF (GGX only) loses energy at grazing in Cycles
(0.1853 at 0.9-0.95 for color 0.5 against 0.25 head-on), as a rough Principled glass does.

Total internal reflection (a rotated cube, white glass, white world, Cycles): Principled 0.9976 mean, Refraction
0.6911 (black where light reflects totally inside), Mix 0.502 0.8295, Mix with the factor max(w, Fresnel > 0.9999)
0.9994 (4.2: 0.8267 -> 0.9994). EEVEE has no total internal reflection (Refraction 1.0): the mask changes nothing there.

### Task 1: build/materials.py

**Files:** Modify `maxdrop/build/materials.py` (module docstring, Reflection weight paragraph; `specular`,
`reflection_weight`; new helpers of this bead `copy_principled`, `refracting`, `refraction_twin`, `tir_weight`;
labels `REFRACTION_LABEL`, `TIR_LABEL`; `TIR_FRESNEL = 0.9999`).

- [ ] `specular`: the lone path for a constant Metallic >= 1, or a constant Transmission Weight >= 1 with `ir.fresnel`
  off (unchanged for Fresnel off); full glass with Fresnel on takes the weight.
- [ ] `copy_principled(bsdf, label, own, y)`: the copy loop of `reflection_weight` (links, values, distribution,
  subsurface method; own {name: value} unlinked).
- [ ] `refracting(bsdf)`: Transmission Weight linked or > 0, and Alpha unlinked >= 1, no emission (Emission Strength
  linked or > 0 with Emission Color linked or not black), Coat Weight and Sheen Weight unlinked 0.
- [ ] `refraction_twin(bsdf)`: the Refraction BSDF (REFRACTION_LABEL, GGX; Color sqrt per channel of a constant Base
  Color or a Gamma node 0.5 (TWIN_LABEL) on its link; thin wall (the BSDF's Thin Wall socket on): Base Color as is,
  IOR 1; Roughness, IOR, Normal the BSDF's link or value); a constant Transmission Weight >= 1: that node; else a Mix
  Shader (TWIN_LABEL; factor the Transmission Weight's link or value) of the Principled twin (Specular IOR Level 0,
  Transmission Weight 0) and the Refraction BSDF.
- [ ] `tir_weight(bsdf, weight)`: Fresnel (TIR_LABEL; IOR and Normal the BSDF's) -> Math GREATER_THAN TIR_FRESNEL ->
  Math MAXIMUM (input 0 the weight, input 1 the mask), both TIR_LABEL; returns MAXIMUM's output. Not for a thin wall.
- [ ] `reflection_weight`: mirror unchanged; otherwise `refracting(bsdf)` -> the refraction twin and the TIR weight,
  else em3.119's twin.
