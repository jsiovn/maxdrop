# Smoother boundary corners Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A converted TurboSmooth or MeshSmooth keeps the corners of an open mesh where 3ds Max 2020 keeps them, so a
half-modelled object smoothed below its Symmetry gets 3ds Max's vertex counts and no sliver at the seam (bead
maxdrop-6n0.6.7).

**Architecture:** `convert/modifiers.py` `_smoother` adds `("boundary_smooth", "PRESERVE_CORNERS")` to the
Subdivision Surface settings (Blender 2.91 and later: 4.2, 4.5 and 5.2 have it); MeshSmooth quad output, which the
probe shows is not Catmull-Clark, becomes a line in the report as classic is. A fixture node pins it.

**Tech Stack:** Python (convert/, no bpy), MAXScript in 3ds Max 2020 (probe, fixture), Blender 4.2/4.5/5.2.

Bead: maxdrop-6n0.6.7

---

## Probe (3ds Max 2020, 3dsmaxbatch; Blender 5.2 import of the same file)

Scene outside the repo (`C:\maxdrop_fixtures\probe\6n0_6_7\probe.ms`): Editable Poly boxes 20 x 20 x 20 (2 width
segments, top and the -X half deleted) and planes 20 x 20; Symmetry X, slice, weld 0.1.

- TurboSmooth and MeshSmooth NURMS keep a boundary vertex with one face (the half box's (0, ±10, 20), a plane's
  corners) and move every other boundary vertex along the boundary (1/8, 6/8, 1/8: (10, -10, 20) -> (8.75, -7.5,
  20)); interior vertices are Catmull-Clark's. That is Subdivision Surface's `boundary_smooth = 'PRESERVE_CORNERS'`:
  the half box's 23 positions are 3ds Max's on 5.2; `'ALL'` (the default) moves the corner to (1.25, -10, 17.5),
  off the plane, and the Mirror cannot weld it (41 vertices, 3ds Max 39).
- With PRESERVE_CORNERS on 5.2: TurboSmooth 1 + Symmetry 39 v 64 t, TurboSmooth 2 + Symmetry 141 v 256 t,
  MeshSmooth NURMS + Symmetry 39 / 64, TurboSmooth + Symmetry + Shell (em3.134's probe) 78 v 152 t (0, 3, 24): all
  3ds Max's (with ALL: 41, 143, 41, 82 / 160 (0, 1, 24)). The other em3.134 probe nodes are unchanged.
- MeshSmooth quad output is another scheme: every boundary vertex stays and the interior corner (10, -10, 0) goes to
  the mean of its edge midpoints (8.33, -6.67, 3.33), not Catmull-Clark's (7.78, -5.56, 4.44). Same counts, other
  positions: a line in the report ("quad output made as Catmull-Clark"), as classic has.
