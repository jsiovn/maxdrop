## Probe results (done before this plan; gamma off unless named; `getPixels ... linear:true`, 9 x 9 means)

### 6n0.6.23

1. Renderer ClassID of other Corona versions: none available. A census of every local `.max` file (370, counts
   only) found one Corona renderer, Corona 12, `(0x62A85DCC, 0x523C3604)`. For comparison, V-Ray keeps one
   renderer ClassID `(0x73BAB286, 0x77F8FD0C)` across a 3.60 save (3ds Max 2015) and 5.2 saves: assumed for Corona,
   not measured.
2. An Output with `map1Enabled` off over a red (200, 50, 50) Checker under Corona (white furnace, no light, specular
   off): in a Standard diffuse slot it renders its map, `0.784 0.196 0.196`, the same as the Output switched on and
   the Checker directly (a white Standard reads 1.0); in a CoronaPhysicalMtl the same (0.745 0.191 0.194 in all
   three). A VRayMtl does not render under Corona 12: a white VRayMtl reads `1.0 0.125 0.125`, its Checker
   `1.0 0.201 0.201`, so its Output cannot be read. MaxDrop's scene-wide rule (the switch not read in a Corona
   scene) stands.
3. Map switches off vs on in a light material's texmap (red Checker as the map, green / blue as the map's own
   colors), V-Ray 5.2 and Corona 12: Mix map1 / map2 / mask, RGB Multiply map1 / map2, Composite layer and layer
   mask, Falloff map1 and map2: Corona renders each exactly as V-Ray (off: the color, or the amount for the mask;
   on: the map). Color Correction has no map switch (nothing to measure). VRayDirt does not render in Corona: white
   `1.0` with its unoccluded map on and off (V-Ray: the map, or the green unoccluded color). The Output control:
   V-Ray white off, Corona the map.

### 6n0.6.30

- Corona 12 renders Hair and Fur strands (a black plane's window mean 0.027 without the modifier, 0.494 with
  white strands), with or without a Hair and Fur render effect and in every `hairMode` (0, 1, 2): the same 0.494.
- The strands take Hair and Fur's own tip / root colors (red: `0.685 0.098 0.026`; grey 128: 0.424; white: 0.494),
  not the growth object's material: a Multi/Sub with a red CoronaLightMtl, a CoronaHairMtl at melanin 0 and one at
  melanin 1 at the strands' MaterialGeomMtlID read 0.494 all alike.
- `MR_ApplyShader` on and `MR_Shader` set to a CoronaColor or a CoronaHairMtl: MAXScript refuses the assignment (a
  system exception); the strands read 0.494.
- So a CoronaHairMtl does not reach Hair and Fur strands through any route tried; Corona shades them from Hair and
  Fur's material parameters. No code (hair curves are not imported).

### 6n0.6.35

CoronaLegacyMtl and CoronaPhysicalMtl, self-illuminated white over black, opacity color white / grey 64 / red
(255, 0, 0) / (200, 100, 50) / black, 64 passes, gamma off and on: the opacity is per channel, the swatch as stored
in both modes: `1.0`, `0.251`, `1.0 0.0 0.0`, `0.784 0.392 0.196`, `0.0`. Not the mean (red would be 0.333). A
float alpha cannot hold it: MaxDrop keeps the mean of the swatch as stored and reports a colored opacity color as an
issue (rule change, with tests); the module docstring says what was measured.
