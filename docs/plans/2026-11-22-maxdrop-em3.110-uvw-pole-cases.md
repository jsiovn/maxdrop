# UVW Map pole cases the uvwmap_pole fixture leaves unpinned Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** pin the four open UVW Map pole cases of bead maxdrop-em3.110 with nodes appended to the uvwmap_pole
fixture, and change `maxdrop/convert/uvwmap.py` only where 3ds Max 2020's map vertices disagree.

**Architecture:** `apply()` gains a center test for spherical maps (r below `CENTER_TOLERANCE`: a pole with V 0.5);
`_seam` moves the corners of a face across the seam down a turn (those above 0.5) instead of up (those below), and a
cylindrical pole stays 0.5 on every face. The tests compare U in whole units after tiling (image-equal), not in
turns times the tile.

**Tech Stack:** plain Python + numpy; MAXScript in 3ds Max 2020 (`3dsmaxbatch.exe` for probes, the dry run and the
oracle; an own interactive `3dsmax.exe` running a wrapper that saves and quits); Blender 4.2 / 4.5 / 5.2 headless.

## Facts (probes 1-3, 3dsmaxbatch, 2026-11-22; scratch folder outside the repo)

Same meshes as uvwmap_pole (cylinder r 8 h 12 10 sides, sphere r 8 10 segments, turned R1 / R2 after 7 degrees
about Z), compared corner by corner with MaxDrop (`convert.scene.read` of the batch-saved probe file):

1. **Spherical bound scales with r**: sphere under a 16 x 16 x 32 gizmo (r = 0.5 at the poles), poles pushed
   (6e-5, 0) radii: 3ds Max gives the angle (U 0.2498 at the pole), as the r-scaled bound does (an absolute 1e-4
   would make it a pole); pushed (4e-5, 0): the face mean. No rule change.
2. **Cylindrical with cap on, cone apex** (Cone radius2 0, welded to one apex: 12 vertices, 20 faces): the apex on
   the side faces gets U 0.5, the base center (a cap face) the planar cap mapping; with U tile 2.5 the apex gets
   1.25 (0.5 x tile). Matches MaxDrop up to the seam turn below.
3. **Sphere center**: a cylinder under a spherical gizmo centered on its top cap center, turned R2: 3ds Max gives
   that corner V 0.5 and U the mean of its face's other corners; MaxDrop got an arbitrary angle (its float64 point
   is 2.2e-16 radii off the center). Pushed off along X by 1e-20 .. 1e-3 radii (untilted, probes 3 and 4): 3ds Max
   gives the angle (V 0.5, U 0.25); along Z a push below half a float ulp of the coordinate 12 is lost when the
   vertex is stored (V 0.5, the face mean), a larger one gives V 1 (probe 5, gizmo sizes 1.6, 16 and 160). So 3ds Max takes the center only at its own exact zero; MaxDrop takes r < 1e-9 (far above its float64
   residue, below anything single precision tells apart from zero at the gizmo's scale). Under a turned gizmo, a
   push of 1e-7 .. 1e-5 radii gives an angle from 3ds Max's single-precision noise: not matched, not fixtured.
4. **Seam with a non-integer U tile** (2.5, also -1.5, cylindrical and spherical): on a face across the seam 3ds
   Max moves the corners above 0.5 down a turn (0.9694 -> -0.0306) and keeps a cylindrical pole at 0.5 (tiled
   1.25); MaxDrop moved the others up (+1, the pole 1.5): a whole turn apart, 2.5 units after tiling: another part
   of the image. With "-1 above 0.5" every non-around face of every probe node matches in whole units; the committed
   fixtures differ from 3ds Max in exactly the same faces as before (faces around the axis, modifiers after the map).
