# Shapes: NGon corner radius, Ellipse outline, rectangular rendering section Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The reader draws an NGon's corner radius and an Ellipse's outline as 3ds Max does, an Arc's `from` / `to`
as 3ds Max clamps them, and a renderable shape with a rectangular section becomes a curve with a rectangular bevel
in Blender; a new fixture `shapes_more` holds every case and its oracle.

**Architecture:** `reader/shapes.py` builds the extra knots (fitted to probes of 3ds Max 2020, then checked against
the fixture's oracle at 1e-4); `veccalc` becomes the SDK's float32 bisection (needed for a zero-length arc, whose
vectors are what the float loop stops at). `Render` gains `section_angle` (block index 14). `convert/scene.py`
gives a CurveDescription a `section` (four profile points in meters, turned by the angle) instead of a round bevel;
`build/curves.py` makes a hidden 2D poly curve object for it, linked into the import's root collection (an unlinked
bevel object has no user and is lost on save, and Esc's cleanup removes only the collection tree), tagged
`maxdrop_helper = "section"`, and sets `bevel_mode = 'OBJECT'`. A section on a curve that is not flat in XY keeps
an issue (Blender's twist may differ from 3ds Max's there).

**Tech Stack:** MAXScript (3ds Max 2020, `3dsmaxbatch.exe`, the ViewCube / solid-background wrapper), plain Python
`unittest`, Blender 4.2 / 4.5 / 5.2 headless.

Bead: maxdrop-em3.48

## Facts measured (3ds Max 2020 probes, outside the repo: `C:\maxdrop_fixtures\probe\em3_48\probe*.txt`)

- **NGon corner radius** (not circular): each corner k (at radius R, angle k * 360 / n) becomes two Bezier knots of
  segment type curve, at distance d = cornerRadius * tan(180 / n) from the corner along the side to the previous
  corner (first) and to the next (second); the first knot of the spline is the one before corner 0. Both vectors
  lie along the side, length d * CIRCLE_VECTOR_LENGTH (0.5517862 = veccalc(90 degrees) in float32): the first
  knot's in vector away from the corner, its out vector toward it; the second's in vector toward the corner, its
  out vector away. No clamp (4 sides, radius 10, corner radius 30: the knots cross, same formula). Scribe changes
  R only. Circular ignores the corner radius (5 sides, circular, corner radius 2: the plain round NGon).
  Checked: 6 sides r 10 cr 2; 3 sides scribe 1 r 10 cr 1.5; 4 sides cr 30.
- **Ellipse outline** (MAXScript `ellipse_outline`, `ellipse_thickness`; ParamBlock2 ids 3 and 2): on, a second
  closed spline, the same construction with both semi-axes plus the thickness, each clamped at 0 (20 x 40 with
  -15: semi-axes 5 and 0; with -25: all knots at the center). On with thickness 0: two coincident splines (the
  flag alone decides the count). Same start and direction as the first.
- **Rectangle** corner radius past half a side (10 x 30 r 8; 10 x 12 r 20): the current construction, unclamped,
  equals 3ds Max's knots.
- **Arc** from / to: both clamped to [0, 360], then `to` below `from` adds 360: 40 -> 40, 400 -> 500, 370 -> 380,
  0 -> -90 are zero-length (all knots at the clamped from), 0 -> 360 a full turn, 40 -> 400 and 10 -> 370 end at
  360, -30 -> 60 starts at 0, 270 -> 90 and 100 -> 50 wrap (180 and 310 degrees). A zero-length arc's vectors are
  1.5 / 1024 x radius long (radius 10 and 1000): the float32 bisection stops after 10 halvings. A float32 port of
  the SDK's veccalc gives 1.46484 at radius 1000, 0.010986 for 0.5 degrees at radius 10 (3ds Max: 0.01098; the
  double version 0.0097), 0.5517862 for 90 degrees and 5.35304 for 72 degrees at radius 12.3607, as 3ds Max.
- **Rectangular section** (render mesh, `snapshotAsMesh renderMesh:true`): a line along +X with width 4, length 1:
  Y +-2, Z +-0.5; along +Z: X +-2, Y +-0.5; a Circle r 10: radial 7.987 .. 12.013 (miter at the polyline points),
  Z +-0.5. Angle (index 14) 30 on the X line: (Y, Z) = (-1.48205, 1.43301), (1.98205, -0.566987),
  (1.48205, -1.43301), (-1.98205, 0.566987); capped (8 vertices, 12 faces).
- **Blender** (4.2.23, 5.2.0, headless): `bevel_mode = 'OBJECT'` with a 2D closed POLY rectangle maps profile x
  to the line's -Y (X line) and to X (Z line), profile y to Z: the rectangle (+-w/2, +-l/2) turned by +angle in the
  profile plane gives 3ds Max's vertices above exactly; the circle gives radial 7.998 .. 12.002, Z +-0.5. The
  profile object hidden in viewport and render, linked in a collection, survives save and reload and still bevels;
  an unlinked one has 0 users and is dropped on save. `fill_mode = 'NONE'` on the profile: it renders nothing.
