# Free Bezier handles, constant velocity, turned scale axes and TCB spins Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** `reader/controllers.py` gives 3ds Max's value at frame 0 between two keys where it kept the linear value
and KEYS_INTERPOLATED (maxdrop-em3.144): free Bezier handles, constant velocity on a Bezier Position, Bezier / TCB
scale keys whose scale axes differ, TCB Rotation turns of a revolution or more, opposite Linear Rotation keys.

**Architecture:** `_between` gains the rules the probes fit; where a probe shows no rule (a turn just short of a
revolution, a free handle with constant velocity, axes whose quaternions point apart) the issue stays. The scene's
frame rate (Config `0x20B0/0x0010`) becomes `Scene.ticks_per_frame`, read in `Scene.from_file`, because a free
handle's length is in frames. New nodes go after the last one of `controllers_tangents.ms`.

**Tech Stack:** 3ds Max 2020 (`3dsmaxbatch.exe` probes outside the repo, an own interactive instance for the
save), plain Python `unittest`, Blender 4.2.23 / 4.5.16 / 5.2 headless.

## Facts measured (probes 1 to 6, scratch folder outside the repo, 2026-11-23)

Points sampled every 0.5 or 1 frame and at ticks -1, 1, 7, 33, 160, 799; formulas fitted in Python against the
samples (MAXScript printed with 9 digits).

- **Free handle** (`0x100000`, per key, its own flag: a free middle key between custom keys fits only that way):
  the stored length is the handle's length in frames of the scene's frame rate, measured in (ticks, stored value)
  space. Handle time extent = `L * ticks_per_frame / sqrt(1 + tangent^2)` (tangent in stored value per tick).
  Exact to 2e-6 on Bezier Float (position, Euler X in radians, ScaleXYZ factor), Position (per axis) and Scale,
  steep tangents (0.2 per tick), handles longer than the segment (60 frames on 50: 1.6e-5), and at 25 fps (192
  ticks per frame; 160 misses by 0.95). Toggling free converts a third into frames (16.6675 for a 50-frame segment).
- **Frame rate:** Config stream `0x20B0/0x0010` = `i32` frames per second (24, 25, 30, 60 in four saves of an
  empty scene; nothing else differs); ticks per frame = 4800 / fps.
- **Constant velocity** (`0x8000`): only on a Bezier Position, only the earlier key's flag (later key alone: plain
  curve; Bezier Scale with it: plain curve; Bezier Float: plain, em3.102). The value cubic with lengths of a third
  (stored lengths ignored: lengths 0.6 / 0.1 / 0.3 on either key fit only that way), cut into 40 chords of equal
  parameter; the value at time fraction f is the point f of the polyline's length along it, linearly between two
  chord ends (1e-5). The three floats after the record's lengths cache a lookup (total, u, length there): not read.
  Constant velocity with a free handle fits neither: stays an issue.
- **Scale axes that differ** (Bezier, TCB, Linear Scale): components by the class's curve, the axis slerp at the
  plain time fraction (AXT3: the eased fraction misses by 0.35), scale matrix as before. Every stored axis pair of
  the probes has a positive dot (3ds Max stores them that way): a pair pointing apart stays an issue.
- **TCB Rotation, turn A >= 2 pi** (stored f32 2 pi included): the segment is cut into a half-turn at each end and
  the turn between. With p(x) = q0 * AngAxis(axis, x) (standard quaternion product, the stored Max quaternions),
  U the eased fraction and h = pi / A: U < h gives squad(q0, out control of q0, p(pi), p(pi), U / h); U > 1 - h
  gives squad(p(A - pi), p(A - pi), in control of q1, p(A), (U - 1 + h) / h) (the control turned to p(A)'s sign);
  between, p(A U). Exact (1e-6) for 360, 400, 450, 760 and 800 degrees, two to four keys, tension, continuity,
  bias, ease. Below a revolution squad as before; between 359 and 360 degrees neither fits (359: squad 3.8e-4 off,
  359.9: both miss): the issue stays for a turn within 10 degrees of a revolution (350 fits squad).
- **Linear Rotation, opposite keys** (1 + dot < 5e-7): q(f) = cos(pi f) q0 + sin(pi f) (-y0, x0, -w0, z0), a
  whole turn about q0's local Z (exact, whatever axis the turn was made about). Between 1 + dot of 4e-7 and 4e-5
  the probes switch branch: 1 + dot below 1e-3 (within about 5 degrees of a revolution) stays an issue.
- Smooth Rotation stores both keys of a revolution in one hemisphere and its controls carry the turn: squad as
  before (350 to 359.999 degrees, 2e-7); its opposite guard stays as an unshown case.
- Lists (frame 0, static): a Scale List with two turned axes applies the later entry first (the other order misses
  by 1.28); a Rotation List entry turning about several axes weighs as the reader does (50 % and 30 %).
- Found, not fixed (a debt bead into maxdrop-6n0.6): list Average (parameter 1 = 1: Position and Scale List
  divide by the weights' sum, Rotation List does something else), Scale List weight on a turned axis (0.48 off),
  out-of-range types (`0x8499`, 30 bytes; cycle, loop, ping pong, linear, relative repeat all wrong today), a
  Linear tangent beside a custom key (the stored tangent is stale: 1.5 off; slope to the neighbour 2e-2 off).
