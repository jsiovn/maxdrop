# Out-of-range types, list Average, Scale List weights and Linear tangents Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use subagents when appropriate to implement this plan. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** `reader/controllers.py` gives 3ds Max's frame-0 value for the four cases maxdrop-em3.144 found
(maxdrop-6n0.6.12): a list's Average switch, the out-of-range types of a keyframe controller, a Scale List weight
on turned axes, a Linear Bezier tangent beside a custom key; an issue wherever no rule fits.

**Architecture:** `_keyframe` reads its keys once into a `_Keys` track that evaluates any time inside the keys,
then maps time 0 through the controller's out-of-range types and range (`0x8499`, `0x3003`). `_between` gets the
key's auto bits (`0x2533` / `0x2534`) for the tangent rules. `_list` returns the Average switch (parameter 1 of
the block) beside the entries. A new fixture `controllers_ranges` (controllers_tangents has 34 KB left).

**Tech Stack:** 3ds Max 2020 (`3dsmaxbatch.exe` probes outside the repo, an own interactive instance for the
fixture save), plain Python `unittest`, Blender 4.2.23 / 4.5.16 / 5.2 headless.

## Facts measured (probes A, B, C, scratch folder outside the repo, 2026-11-24)

Samples every frame from -80 to 130, compared in Python with the reader's in-range curve.

- **Out-of-range types:** a container `0x8499` beside the keys (in `0x7127` for a Bezier Float) holds `0x3000`
  before and `0x3001` after (i32: 2 cycle, 3 loop, 4 ping pong, 5 linear, 7 relative repeat; 0 for MAXScript's
  #identity; no chunk for constant) and `0x3003` (i32 1 = ORTs switched off: constant). Setting them on a Position
  XYZ writes them on its three floats too. The controller's `0x3003` (two i32) is its range, the keys' first and
  last time unless the range bar was moved. Outside the range [r0, r1] (period T = r1 - r0): cycle and loop take
  `r1 - (r0 - t) mod T` before and `r0 + (t - r1) mod T` after (a whole period before r0 lands on r1); ping pong
  mirrors; relative repeat adds k times (value(r1) - value(r0)) per period crossed (a rotation: v * (v0^-1 v1)^k;
  a scale: its components); linear extends the slope of one tick at the range end (`value(r0 + 1) - value(r0)`;
  a rotation `v * (a^-1 b)^ticks`), within the f32 noise 3ds Max's own one-tick difference has (2e-4 ten frames
  out). Inside the range but outside the keys: the nearest key's value; outside the range with constant: the value
  at the range end (a range of 20 to 40 inside keys at 10 to 50 gives frame 20's value at frame 0). Every
  keyframe class (Linear, Bezier, TCB Float, Position, Rotation, Scale; Smooth Rotation) within 1e-5 but linear.
  Loop over a range wider than the keys on both sides: a straight line from the last key's value to the first's
  across the gap (a Bezier Float, every frame of three gaps within 2.4e-7). Not fitted: Loop over a range moved
  off the keys otherwise, #identity (the whole controller reads zero, a scale 0, inside the keys too), ORTs
  switched off beside a moved range (not probed).
- **List Average** (parameter 1 of the list's block, i32): Float and Position List: the weighted sum divided by the
  weights' sum, 0 when that sum is 0 (one entry at 40 %: its value); Scale List of plain scales: the weighted mean
  of the components, identity when the sum is 0; Rotation List: slerp from identity to each entry in turn by its
  weight (`acc = slerp(acc, q_k, w_k)`; 11 probes exact, weights 0 to 2). A Scale List average with a turned axis
  fits none of the tried rules (0.59 off): an issue.
- **Scale List weights:** one entry turned at any weight (60, 30, 150, -50 %) and plain entries at any weights fit
  `I + (S - I) w` per entry, a later one first; a weight other than 100 % on an entry applied after a turned one (or
  a turned one after a non-identity scale) fits none of the tried rules (0.2 to 0.5 off): an issue.
- **Bezier tangents:** `0x2533` and `0x2534` are bit arrays (`0x2700`: i32 count, then a bit per key, LSB first) of
  the keys whose in and out tangent is auto (type 5 like custom), `0x2532` their union. A handle's stored length is
  used only for a custom tangent (type 5, its auto bit clear) or a free handle; every other type takes a third
  (smooth, linear, fast, slow and auto keys store 0.3333 or 0.333: up to 8.7e-3 off). A Linear tangent whose
  neighbour across the segment has a custom or auto tangent on that side is the chord's slope, a third long
  (the stored one is stale: 1.5 off); beside a smooth, fast, slow or linear tangent the stored one is right. Every
  Bezier node of probes A to C and of em3.144's probes within 4.3e-5.
